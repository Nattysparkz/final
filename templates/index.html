🧠 AI Delay Confidence

Enter a travel date to get the JAX neural network's delay prediction and confidence rating for the full Manchester–Euston route on that day.

Understanding the metrics:
Predicted Delay — estimated total minutes of delay for that day, based on learned historical patterns.
Stress Coefficient — a 0–100% measure of route pressure; above 60% signals a high-risk travel day.
Model RMSE — the JAX model's average prediction error in minutes (lower = more accurate). A lower RMSE means you can trust the forecast more.
